About this Monograph

"A profound exploration of the path to spiritual fulfillment, this work has been translated into more than 165 languages, helping millions find hope."

First published in 1892, Steps to Christ has served as a beacon for those navigating the complexities of faith. Ellen G. White avoids the dense theological jargon often found in spiritual literature, opting instead for a warm, accessible narrative that addresses the core of the human experience.

The book meticulously outlines the "steps" to a meaningful connection with Christ: confession, repentance, faith, and the daily growth of the spirit. Each chapter serves as a contemplative study, designed to be read slowly and revisited often.
